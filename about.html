01 / Personality

A little quieter.
Still very much involved.

I'm calm, observant, and open to other people's perspectives. Socialising isn't usually how I choose to spend my energy, but I can adapt to a party, a workplace, or a new team.

I tend to listen before I speak. I won't usually interrupt a conversation unless I have something interesting or funny to add. My voice can be a little hard to hear, so it's completely fine to ask me to repeat something.

I pay attention to the people around me and try to read the room. In a team, I care about being considerate and helping people work together without unnecessary friction.

Working with me

Be honest with me.

I value straightforward feedback over being reassured that everything is fine. If I'm doing something wrong, tell me. It helps me learn faster and saves us both from running into the same problem again.

Empty promises frustrate me. I'd much rather hear a clear, honest answer, even when it isn't the answer I was hoping for.
